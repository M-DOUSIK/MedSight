# [1] WHO 2003 - Adherence to Long-Term Therapies

**Claim it supports:** long-term medication adherence is poor at population
scale.

**The number:** ~50% average adherence among patients with chronic disease in
developed countries. Lower in developing countries.

**The caveat:** a 2003 synthesis across many conditions, not a measurement of
one population. Use it to establish that the problem is large and
long-recognised. Do not use it to size anything.

**Used in:** the ~500-word description; slide 1.
