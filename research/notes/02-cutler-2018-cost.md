# [2] Cutler et al. 2018 - economic impact of non-adherence

**Claim it supports:** non-adherence carries a large but poorly-bounded cost.

**The number:** $949-$44,190 per person per year, disease-specific, 2015 US$,
across 79 studies and 14 disease groups. All-cause figures ran $5,271-$52,341.

**The caveat, which is the whole reason this source was chosen over a bigger
headline:** the review's own conclusion is that the evidence is limited and of
varying quality, that methods differ enough to make studies incomparable, and
that an accurate estimate of the true magnitude is *impossible*. Only 10 of 79
studies rated high quality.

**Therefore:** quote the range and the uncertainty. Never a single national
total. A source that reports that its own field cannot yet produce a reliable
number is being used honestly only if that finding is passed on with it.
