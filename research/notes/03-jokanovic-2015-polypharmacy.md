# [3] Jokanovic et al. 2015 - polypharmacy in long-term care

**Claim it supports:** the population MedSight targets takes many medications
at once. This is the direct measure of the single-turntable limitation.

**The number:** across 44 studies - up to **91%** of long-term-care residents
take more than 5 medications, **74%** more than 9, **65%** more than 10.

**The caveat:** "up to" is an upper bound across heterogeneous studies, not a
central estimate. Prevalence varied widely by study, facility, country, and by
which definition of polypharmacy was used (5, 9 or 10 medications). Write it as
an upper bound every time it appears.

**Why it matters here:** MedSight has one turntable and therefore serves one
medication, while two thirds of its target population take ten or more. That is
the number the stacked-module work is aimed at.
