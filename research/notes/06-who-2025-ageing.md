# [6] WHO 2025 - ageing worldwide

**Claim it supports:** the population this device is for is growing everywhere,
not in one country.

**The numbers:**

- By 2030, **1 in 6 people worldwide** will be aged 60 or over.
- The 60-plus population goes from **1 billion in 2020 to 1.4 billion in
  2030**, and doubles to **2.1 billion by 2050**.
- The number of people aged 80 or over is expected to **triple between 2020
  and 2050**, to 426 million.

**Source:** World Health Organization, *Ageing and health* fact sheet,
published 1 October 2025.
<https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ageing-and-health>

**The caveat:** these are projections from the UN World Population Prospects,
not measurements. Quote them as projections. They are also averages across
very different countries: the share is already far higher in some and far
lower in others, so "1 in 6 worldwide by 2030" is a statement about the world,
not about anywhere in particular.

**Used in:** the handbook's opening, the submission site, slide 1.
