# [7] WHO 2025 - the health workforce shortfall

**Claim it supports:** care work is the constraint on top of the ageing
population, and it is a global constraint.

**The number:** a projected shortfall of about **11 million health workers by
2030**, concentrated in low- and lower-middle-income countries.

**Source:** World Health Organization, health workforce programme.
<https://www.who.int/health-topics/health-workforce>

**The caveat:** the projection has moved as the method has: 18 million in
2016, 15 million in 2022, about 10-11 million for 2030. Quote the current
figure as a projection and as approximate, and do not present the fall from 18
to 11 million as a shortage being solved - the definition and the model
changed too.

**Used in:** the handbook's opening.
